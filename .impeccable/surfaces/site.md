## Direction contract

THESIS: Scripture treated like a bold illuminated capital worth flooding the page in gold ink, not a delicate serif quote — refuses both the soft pastel "Christian merch" default and the neutral black/cream/gold luxury-editorial default already built and rejected this project.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ink ground throughout. One committed flooded gold ink field (a dominant graphic color, not an accent line) carries 30-60% of key surfaces. A single crimson "rubrication" red marks emphasis and verse references, echoing how manuscripts highlighted key words in red. Oversized flat "illuminated capital" letterforms act as graphic anchors (built as bold geometric graphic blocks, never delicate calligraphy). Confident slab/grotesk display type for headlines, not a delicate serif. Dense radial gold linework used as bold flat background fields behind hero product shots, never thin decorative line art.

STORY: The visitor understands immediately that this is streetwear treating faith with real graphic weight and confidence — not a gift shop, not generic minimal luxury fashion — and is moved to shop because the product itself looks like something worth wearing loud.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed hero product photo (existing model shot). A massive flat gold illuminated-capital graphic bleeds off one edge, behind/around the model. Verse reference set in red rubrication type. Tagline in bold slab display type. Primary CTA anchored lower-left, high contrast against the black ground.

FORM: Illuminated Manuscript / Gold Ink direction — dealt lead (index 4, "THE ROLL") of concept-seed key e1628b5d, chosen by the user over the two other dealt structures (Hand-Painted Indian Signage; Devotional Journal / Margin Notes). Signature interaction borrows the ukiyo-e woodblock challenger's registered-layer discipline: content reveals in registered color passes on scroll — black keyline first, then the gold field floods in, then red rubrication marks land last — like a print pulling into registration, rather than a soft opacity fade.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
